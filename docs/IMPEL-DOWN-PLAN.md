# Impel Down: island plan

Prepared 26 October 2026. Implemented locally on the same date; see [implementation notes](IMPEL-DOWN-IMPLEMENTATION.md) for the delivered scope, inspected floor references and measured results. The proposals and reference questions below retain the original planning baseline. Amazon Lily is also implemented locally.

## 1. Direction and historical state

A monumental prison rising from the Calm Belt, with a submerged stepped structure that becomes wider toward its base. The experience begins at its fortified entrance, reveals the depth through an architectural cutaway, then descends through distinct environments. Strong silhouettes, readable masonry and carefully lit interiors should carry the detail; a pile of identical circular rooms would not capture this location.

Model the intact prison around Luffy's infiltration. Story cards can recount the later breakout without simultaneously staging every damaged wall, poison attack and escaped prisoner. Magellan is the warden in this reconstruction. Newkama Land represents Ivankov's refuge during this visit, not its later leadership. Ace has already been transferred when Luffy reaches Level 6: do not stage a successful rescue there. Marineford is a separate destination.

Use empty architectural scenes and environmental storytelling initially. Character combat, torture animation, moving prisoner crowds and a simulated prison-wide escape are outside the first build. A Marine vessel suits the approach; Sunny and Merry do not belong here.

Narrative foundation: [official Impel Down overview](https://one-piece.com/story/impel_down/index.html), [infiltration, episode 422](https://one-piece.com/anime/422/index.html), [Level 6 arrival, 442](https://one-piece.com/anime/442/index.html), [escape to a Marine ship, 451](https://one-piece.com/anime/451/index.html). Some official pages reject direct requests; indexed official summaries were available. Final cards require a verified source for their specific event. Do not treat every episode in the arc's numbered range as prison action: tie-ins, crew updates and recaps interrupt that range.

### Resolve before detailed modeling

1. Wide and close views of each floor: blade forest/cell relationship on 1; beast corridors and gates on 2; dry cells and shafts on 3; cauldron, bridges and furnaces on 4; frozen cell blocks on 5; heavy cells and main passage on 6.
2. The complete Newkama room and its concealed access. Its small hidden footprint must fit between the lower floors without becoming a seventh full-width tier.
3. Surface side/rear views, Marine vessel profile, mooring arrangement and entrance insignia. Verify silhouette and lettering before sculpting relief.
4. Stairs, elevators, ventilation and security routes. Only model connections supported by references; designate connective passages as interpretive where necessary.
5. The Gate of Justice approach and control-room location. The huge sea gate and prison entrance are different structures. Do not pin Bon Clay's control-room event to an invented floor.

Search captions are insufficient identification: unrelated Udon prisons, Dressrosa factory diagrams and fan height comparisons appeared in results. No exact physical dimensions are established here.

## 3. Three coordinated views

**Exterior:** the intact fortress, approach water and vessel. Preserve the crown emblem, tall gate, masonry tiers and correctly shaped sails/rigging. A distant Gate of Justice is optional until its relationship to the approach is established; never attach it to the entrance facade.

**Section overview:** a deliberate architectural cutaway exposes the stacked interior. The removed face is a presentation device, not a hole flooding the prison. Keep a clear sea-level reference, back walls, floor rims and the surrounding deep-blue field. Avoid transparent walls and an ocean surface drawn across the exposed rooms.

**Focused floor:** show one environment at useful scale. Authored near-wall and roof groups lift from view; retain the room's supporting structure. Hide inactive detail and effects. Provide enough light for cells, props and passages to remain readable on a phone without flattening all floors to the same color.

All three are states within one scene. Floor selection, cutaway changes and story navigation use short camera/visibility transitions without replaying the voyage loader. Reduced motion snaps to a valid composed state.

### Water and depth prototype

Exterior mode uses the existing ocean approach with a quieter Calm Belt profile. Section/floor mode explicitly hides or masks foreground water and disables inappropriate exterior fog; a camera below sea level must not see the underside of the ocean crossing a room. Recompute relevant cached depth when shell visibility changes. Moving vessel bounds remain separate from static depth data.

Prototype this before detailing all floors. A working exterior ocean alone does not validate submerged interior presentation.

## 6. Cameras, interaction and mobile composition

Extend the established `MeasuredIslandCamera` approach only where a floor-aware travel route requires it. A direct linear move from Level 1 to Level 6 would cross floors: route outward through the exposed face, descend through an unobstructed corridor, then approach the selected room. Store and inspect intermediate waypoints for arbitrary floor jumps, returns and rapid repeated selection. Do not permit orbit limits that push the camera into a ceiling or closed wall.

Use existing landmark navigation with clear level names and a compact current-level indicator. If a floor picker is needed, make it a single accessible control; avoid a permanent second sidebar. Show only relevant markers in a focused floor. Return, Escape, reverse wheel and downward swipe restore the previous journey position and presentation state. Brand returns to the atlas; loading travel remains reserved for initial loading and actual island changes.

At blockout, test surface, furnace, refuge and Level 6 on 390×740 and 320×568. Fit the model in the measured space between header, story and dock. Author separate phone approaches where necessary. Cards retain readable type, 44px controls and complete actions without internal scrolling. If content cannot fit, shorten/split the story instead of clipping actions or shrinking text below the established readable scale.

Review every landmark at 1440×900 and 390×740, plus 360×640 and 844×390 layout extremes, enlarged text and reduced motion. Dark environments still need clear silhouettes and sufficient text contrast. The existing atlas entry `impel` remains informational until a future model is ready.

## 7. Detail, atmosphere and performance targets

Use masonry families, varied bars, hinges, chains, torches, pipes, bridge supports and sparse props appropriate to each floor. Make recognizability come from the architecture and environment. Avoid identical rooms distinguished only by colored lights.

- Bake static interior illumination; use restrained emissive surfaces for the furnace and lamps. Do not add a dynamic light to every torch.
- Activate only the selected environment's ambient effect: capped embers on 4 or sparse snow on 5, never a prison-wide particle storm. Plan at most 48 active particles in a small number of instanced draws.
- Newkama Land needs warm inhabited detail and enough surrounding rock to read as concealed. Leave clear access and camera space around furniture.
- Keep the moored ship nearly still. Any bob must be bounded and checked using full sails, hull and rigging against the gate, dock and water, including intermediate poses.
- Reduced motion freezes effects and vessels in valid poses. No automatic combat spectacle or constant thunderstorm.

These are planning targets, not measurements:

| Item | Initial budget |
| --- | --- |
| Entire static model | 180k–240k triangles |
| Allocation at the upper bound | Surface 35k; six main floors 150k; refuge 15k; vessel/gate/connecting detail 40k |
| Total visibility/material batches | 70–110; aim for 15–35 active architecture batches in a focused floor |
| Baked model download | 12–18 MiB |
| Lighting texture | One 4096px atlas initially; approximately 85.3 MiB decoded RGBA with mipmaps, excluding geometry and other GPU allocations |
| Whole-frame balanced-quality goal | Under 100 calls at normal focused stops; measure exterior/overview and depth passes separately |

Use the existing Cycles-to-unlit asset pipeline, retained split corner normals, explicit UV margins and weighted texel allocation for close-view walls, emblems and furniture. Verify the atlas across all floors before increasing resolution. An additional 4096px atlas would materially increase memory and needs a budget reassessment, not a silent quality fix.

Hiding floors reduces drawing, not resident geometry/texture memory. Do not describe visibility filtering as streaming. Record final bytes, triangles, batches, decoded texture estimates and actual frame samples separately from these targets. Real-phone FPS remains unmeasured until tested on named hardware.

## 8. Implementation sequence and acceptance gates

1. Resolve missing floor and circulation references; distinguish observed features from authored connective detail.
2. Prototype a rough exterior plus Level 4, the 5.5 pocket and Level 6. Prove water separation, shell visibility, readable lighting, mobile framing and transitions before expanding the model.
3. Block out the complete stepped structure, scale anchors and all fourteen stops. Review every floor footprint and intermediate camera route.
4. Build the entrance, cauldron, refuge and deepest cells first; then fill other environments with their verified identifying features. Complete vessel detail only after dock bounds work.
5. Bake and review close-up clarity, seams, bars, flame surfaces, snow edges and floor-to-floor contrast. Measure asset costs and remove hidden excess.
6. Integrate sourced stories, atlas entry and navigation. Exercise rapid floor selection, exterior/section switches, all return paths, missing-model recovery and cached re-entry. Check other islands if shared components change.
7. Visually review every stop on desktop and phone, including ordinary motion, reduced motion and smallest supported layouts. Capture and fix failures before declaring completion. Compilation and DOM checks alone do not establish visual acceptance.
